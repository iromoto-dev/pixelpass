What happens to your picture

  1. It's laid on a grid: at pixel size 2, each new pixel covers 2 × 2 of the original.
  2. Each pixel takes the colour of the larger half of its cell, light or dark, so edges stay sharp. Small details that stand out, like a lit window, are kept.
  3. Contrast and saturation get a small lift, then a palette is picked from the picture, or you choose a fixed one.
  4. Smooth gradients get an ordered dither; edges get one solid colour.
  5. Stray specks blend into their surroundings, unless they stand out like a star.

Is it real pixel art?

In format, yes: every pixel sits on the grid in one of a few colours, so it scales up crisp at any whole multiple.

In craft, no: an algorithm placed the pixels, so busy areas keep some speckle an artist would clean up. Use it as a strong starting point.
